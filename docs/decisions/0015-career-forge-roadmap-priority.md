# ADR 0015: Career Forge is Friday's first flagship specialization

- Status: accepted product direction
- Date: 2026-09-10

## Context

The earlier roadmap made Market Intelligence/Trading Research, Cognitive
Amplification, and Creator Studio mandatory Stages 20–22. The owner finalized a
different priority: Friday's first specialization must make the owner a capable,
job-ready ML/AI Engineer through demonstrated, retained engineering ability.

## Decision

Keep Stages 0–12 and their accepted history. The active order after Stage 12 is:
Persistent Memory (13), Career Forge Core V1 (14), perception (15), desktop
control (16), autonomous execution (17), events (18), role orchestration (19),
self-learning/research (20), cognitive amplification (21), and Career Forge
Advanced Integration (22).

Market Intelligence/Trading Research and Creator Studio/Digital Media
Intelligence are deferred future specialization ideas outside the active roadmap.
They are retained with their historical detail, but receive no implementation
effort or implied trading/publication authority unless the owner explicitly
restores them. The detailed Career Forge contract is architectural policy in
`docs/architecture/career-forge.md`.

## Consequences

Stage 14 cannot begin before accepted Stage 13 memory. Career Forge preserves
Local Intelligence Sovereignty and the one-current-general-model policy. It is
not a course/quiz/LeetCode/JD-matching/resume-generator product; GitHub activity
is evidence of real learning rather than a target. Historical records retain the
superseded roadmap decision so future sessions can distinguish deferral from
silent deletion.
